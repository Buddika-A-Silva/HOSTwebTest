The Leo Club of Colombo (Host), established in 1969, is a prestigious community-based Omega Leo Club sponsored by the Lions Club of Colombo. It focuses on community service and leadership development, engaging in various initiatives aimed at the betterment of the community, including childcare, eldercare, and educational projects. The club emphasizes teamwork, innovation, and compassion, fostering personal growth and meaningful contributions to society. For more information, you can visit their official LinkedIn page.

The Leo Club of IIT is a dynamic community
of young leaders dedicated to service,
leadership, and growth. As part of the world’s
largest youth service movement, we
empower students to create positive change,
develop leadership skills, and build lasting
connections—locally and globally. Together,
we turn passion into action.

Our Vision

To inspire and empower young individuals at IIT to
become compassionate leaders who drive
meaningful change in their communities and
beyond.

Our Mission

To develop leadership, foster fellowship, and serve
humanity by creating impactful projects, nurturing
personal growth, and building a strong network of
socially responsible youth.
