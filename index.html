About Us

The Leo Club of Colombo (Host), established in 1969, is a legacy as you become part of. As you step into this prestigious club, sponsored by the Lions Club of Colombo, you join a long-standing tradition of leadership, compassion, and impactful service in Sri Lanka.

When you become a Leo here, you don’t just volunteer—you contribute to decades of meaningful change. You take part in initiatives that uplift communities through childcare projects, elder support programs, educational empowerment, and countless other service efforts designed to make lives better. Every project gives you the chance to lead, to create, and to inspire those around you. What makes this club truly special is the environment you become a part of. You’ll work with dedicated, passionate young leaders who embody teamwork, innovation, and empathy. Along the way, you’ll discover your own strengths, develop new skills, and grow into someone who can make a real difference.
